Queria vs. Quero

Queria (conditional — I would like) is far more polite than quero (I want). Always use queria in a restaurant — it's the standard register. Same as "I'd like" vs "I want" in English, but the gap feels even more marked in Portuguese.

Recomendaria — Conditional Tense

recomendaria is the conditional of recomendar — "would recommend." You don't need to produce this yet, but you'll hear it constantly in restaurants. The pattern: Que ___ recomendaria? — What ___ would you recommend?
