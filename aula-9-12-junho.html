Advérbios de Lugar — aqui · aí · ali · cá · lá
AdvérbioEnglishNota
aquihere (near speaker)A Sofia está aqui. — perto do falante
aíthere (near listener)O livro está aí? — perto do ouvinte
aliover there (far from both)O gato está ali! — longe de todos
cáhere (general / in this country)Moro cá com a minha família.
láthere (general / in that country)Ela está lá de férias com a família.
cá vs aqui · lá vs ali

aqui / aí / ali are precise — they indicate the speaker's exact physical reference point.
cá / lá are vague/general — used for countries, regions, general whereabouts. Moro cá = I live here (in Portugal). Ela está lá = She's there (in another country).

Expressões — Diz-se Assim!
Diz-se Assim
Para mim.
For me. / As for me. / In my opinion.
Used when expressing a personal opinion or preference. Para mim, o melhor dia é a sexta. — For me, Friday is the best day.
Diz-se Assim
O que lá vai, lá vai.
What's gone is gone. / What's done is done.
Said to encourage someone — we can't change the past, better to look forward. Equivalent to "let it go" or "water under the bridge."

Plural Forms — recognize but don't produce yet

este/esta → estes/estas
esse/essa → esses/essas
aquele/aquela → aqueles/aquelas

You'll hear and read these often. For now focus on the singular forms.

EP vs BR — Important Difference

Brazilian Portuguese often uses esse where EP uses este — Brazilian collapses the near-speaker/near-listener distinction. Don't let Brazilian resources confuse the system above, which is the European standard your instructor is teaching.
